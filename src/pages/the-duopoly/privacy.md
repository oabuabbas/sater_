## In short


<ul>
<li>There is no account. We do not run a server that receives your game, your names or your progress.</li>
<li>Your company, saves and settings are stored on your own device.</li>
<li>The game shows advertisements. Our advertising partner collects some device information to show, measure and protect those ads, as described below.</li>
<li>The game is not directed at children under 13.</li>
</ul>


## 1. Information stored only on your device


<p>To let you continue where you left off, the game keeps the following in your device's local app storage:</p>
<ul>
<li>Your campaign: the founder and company names you type, your in-game finances, staff, family and story choices, and up to three weekly rewind checkpoints.</li>
<li>Your settings: music, sound effects, vibration, volume, and whether you have seen the guide.</li>
</ul>
<p>This information never leaves your device unless you choose <strong>Export save</strong>, which creates a file that you control. We cannot see, recover or delete it for you. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage removes it.</p>
<p>The names you type are fictional game names. Please do not enter real personal details in them.</p>


## 2. Advertising


<p>The Duopoly is free and supported by advertising. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). When an ad is requested or shown, the advertising partner may automatically collect:</p>

<table>
<thead><tr><th>Information</th><th>Used for</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID</td><td>Showing and measuring ads, frequency limits, fraud prevention, and personalised ads where you allow them</td></tr>
<tr><td>IP address and approximate location derived from it</td><td>Serving ads relevant to your region, fraud prevention</td></tr>
<tr><td>Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)</td><td>Showing ads that work on your device, diagnostics</td></tr>
<tr><td>Ad interactions (impressions, taps)</td><td>Measuring ad performance, fraud prevention</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<p>This information is collected and processed by the advertising partner under its own policies, not stored by us. See <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites">how Google uses information from apps that use its services</a> and the <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google Privacy Policy</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Your choices.</strong> Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice in the game's settings. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings under <em>Google › Ads</em>. You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you.</p>


## 3. Permissions


<ul>
<li><strong>Internet</strong> — required to request and show advertisements and to open the research links in the Market section.</li>
<li><strong>Vibration</strong> — for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off.</li>
<li><strong>Advertising ID</strong> — used by the advertising partner as described above.</li>
</ul>
<p>The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or files.</p>


## 4. Links to other websites


<p>The Market section links to public research pages (for example, the Federal Reserve and the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics). Those sites have their own privacy practices.</p>


## 5. Children


<p>The Duopoly is a business simulation intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow personalised advertising to be shown to them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, contact us and we will help.</p>


## 6. Data sharing and sale


<p>We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising partner described in section 2, we do not share information with anyone, because we do not receive it.</p>


## 7. Security and retention


<p>Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Information collected by the advertising partner is retained under its policies. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we do not transmit your game data at all.</p>


## 8. Your rights


<p>Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because your game data is only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. For information held by the advertising partner, use the controls linked in section 2, or contact us and we will direct your request.</p>


## 9. Changes to this policy


<p>If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you inside the game.</p>
