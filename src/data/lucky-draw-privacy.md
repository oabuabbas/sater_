This Privacy Policy explains how the **Lucky Draw** mobile application ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Lucky Draw is a local decision-wheel, prize-draw and team-splitter utility that works offline without registration, and we do not operate backend servers that receive your personal data.

## 1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect or store on our servers your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following locally on your device so your wheels and drafts persist between sessions:

- custom spin wheels, emoji icons, colours, slice options and weights
- draft entrant lists and team-splitter inputs you paste or generate
- spin history counts and past prize-draw result cards (including timestamps and local draw verification codes)
- app preferences (language, sound, theme) and ad-free status

This information never leaves your device through the app and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## 3. On-device motion sensor (optional Shake to spin)

On the wheel screen, you may optionally toggle **Shake to spin** on. When enabled, the app reads linear acceleration data locally in memory solely to detect a physical shake gesture and trigger a wheel spin. Sensor readings are discarded immediately, never stored, and never transmitted off your device.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free app displays ads provided by **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC. To serve, personalize (where permitted) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect:

- your device's advertising ID
- device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language)
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- ad interaction data (such as ad impressions and taps)

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

**Your choices:**

- In regions where consent is required (including the EEA, UK and Switzerland), a consent dialog is shown before personalized ads are used. You can update your choice at any time from **Settings › Privacy options**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (**Settings › Google › Ads** or **Settings › Privacy › Ads**).
- Rewarded video ads for temporary ad-free access are always optional and triggered only when you choose to watch one.

## 5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The lifetime ad-removal purchase (`remove_ads_lifetime`) is processed by **Google Play Billing**. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details. The app only receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play to unlock the ad-free status on your Google account. See [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice).

## 6. Ratings and sharing

- **In-app review:** the app may occasionally display Google Play's native rating prompt. Any review goes strictly to Google Play, not to us.
- **Share result card:** tapping share generates a PNG result image on your device and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is transmitted unless you explicitly pick a destination app.

## 7. Permissions

The app requests internet, network-state and advertising-ID permissions solely to display ads and verify Google Play purchases. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, call-log or precise-location permissions.

## 8. Children

Lucky Draw is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data in connection with the app, contact us and we will help address it.

## 9. Data security and retention

Data saved by the app remains strictly on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for AdMob ads or Google Play Billing is retained under Google's own privacy policies linked above.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on applicable laws (such as GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, erase or restrict processing of personal data. Because we do not hold user data on our own servers, local data can be deleted directly by clearing app data, and ad-related preferences can be managed in Settings or with Google directly. You may also contact us for assistance.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates take effect when published on this page with an updated effective date.
