

**Effective date:** November 6, 2026
**Developer:** SATER
**App:** Find the Spy (Android, package `com.sater.findthespy`)

This Privacy Policy explains how the Find the Spy mobile game ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Find the Spy is a party game played on a single device. It does not require an account, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data.

## 1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect or store on our servers your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following locally on your device so the game works between sessions:

- player names and emoji avatars you enter
- custom word packs you create
- game settings (language, sound, theme, family mode, round options)
- the leaderboard, recent round history and recently used words
- items unlocked in the shop and your ad-free time

This information never leaves your device through the app and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC. To serve, personalize (where permitted) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect:

- your device's advertising ID
- device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language)
- IP address and the approximate location derived from it
- ad interaction data (for example, ad impressions and taps)

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Your choices:**

- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where consent is required, a consent form is shown before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice at any time from **Settings › Privacy options** in the app.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (**Settings › Google › Ads** or **Settings › Privacy › Ads**).
- Rewarded video ads are always optional. You choose whether to watch one.

## 4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases such as removing ads or unlocking shop items are processed by **Google Play Billing**. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details. The app only receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play, which it verifies on your device to unlock the item. Google's handling of payment information is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice: https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice

## 5. Ratings and sharing

- **In-app review:** after some games the app may show Google Play's built-in rating prompt. Any rating or review you submit goes to Google Play, not to us.
- **Share result:** the "Share result" button creates an image of the round's result on your device and opens your device's share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share it with.

## 6. Permissions

The app uses the internet and network-state permissions only to load ads and process purchases, and the advertising ID permission for ads as described above. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

## 7. Children

Find the Spy is a family party game intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A **Family mode** option hides topics that may not suit younger players. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

## 8. Data security and retention

Data stored by the app stays on your device and is kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for ads and purchases is retained according to Google's policies linked above.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold your personal data on our servers, most requests are handled directly on your device (by clearing app data) or with Google through the links above. You can also contact us for help.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
